Profile photos can be restricted to mutual dating matches: a checkbox in the upload dialog keeps a photo out of every response except the profile detail view of a dating, mutually compatible viewer. Eligible viewers see the gallery in the owner's order; everyone else, and the owner's own avatar, gets the first unrestricted photo as the primary. Restricted photos are marked in the owner's gallery, and the "view as" dating toggle previews each audience's gallery.
