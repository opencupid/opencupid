Profile photos can be restricted to mutual dating matches: a checkbox in the upload dialog keeps a photo out of every response except the profile detail view of a dating, mutually compatible viewer. The primary photo is now the first unrestricted one. Restricted photos are marked in the owner's gallery, and the "view as" dating toggle previews each audience's gallery.
