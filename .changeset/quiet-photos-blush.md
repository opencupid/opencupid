Profile photos can be restricted to mutual dating matches: a checkbox in the upload dialog keeps a photo out of every response except the profile detail view of a dating, mutually compatible viewer. The first photo a viewer gets is the primary, for the owner and eligible viewers too, even when it is restricted. Restricted photos are marked in the owner's gallery, and the "view as" dating toggle previews each audience's gallery.
